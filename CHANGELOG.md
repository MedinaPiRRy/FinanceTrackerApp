# Changelog

Version 1.3.0 collects everything since 1.2.0. Earlier versions are described on the GitHub releases page.

## 1.3.0
- **Eight app styles.** Settings → Appearance now has a picker with a live preview card for each style: Light, Dark, Business, GirlMath, Programmer, Cozy, Casual and ADHD (calm, with strong colour and weight kept for amounts owed, over-budget items, transactions waiting for review and each page's main button), plus Match system. Charts follow the style, the choice is remembered and applied before the first paint, and every style is checked for readable contrast by an automated test. The top-bar Light/Dark button becomes a Style button when another style is in use.
- **Taxes & interest, rebuilt.** The page shows what you paid and what you received in taxes and in interest, with the net, a chart and a table by month. Money in counts: GST/HST credits and government payments that are part of your taxes are *received*; refunds reduce what you paid. Click a figure, a category or an account to see the payments behind it, 50 at a time. Mark any payment as taxes or interest (from the page's *Add payments…*, or Transactions → tick rows → *Taxes & interest*), leave a payment, a whole category or a whole account out with one click, and put it back from *Left out*. Income categories can be marked in Settings → Categories too. A bank fee in "Bank Fees & Interest" is no longer counted as interest (only lines that say interest, categories you mark and payments you mark are), and "no interest" / "interest-free" lines are not counted.
- **Restore from a backup.** Settings → Backup & data lists your backups and puts one back (type RESTORE; the data you have now is saved first). Automatic daily backups are now on by default (last 10 kept).
- **Erase means erase.** "Erase all my data" and "Delete the app and its data" now also remove every other backup, upgrade copy and the sample files (only the one backup you ask to keep stays).
- **Faster on big databases.** With 100,000 transactions the dashboard opens in about 0.4 s (was 0.9 s) and Monthly review in 0.5 s (was 3.6 s). A spreadsheet that claims a million rows, or a zip that unpacks to gigabytes, is refused instead of freezing the app.
- **Smaller fixes.** Names that differ only by accents or case are the same name; impossible dates (30 February) are refused; failed upgrades roll back cleanly; plain error messages instead of internal ones; a failed background request now shows a message; a loan link that failed during an import is reported.
- **Fifty rows at a time, everywhere.** Every list and table (Transactions, Review, Import, Budget, Cash, Recurring, Insights, Goals, Accounts, Settings lists and more) shows 50 rows with page controls at the bottom, and remembers the page when you come back with Back.
- **Move, edit or delete many transactions.** Settings → Categories has a "Transactions" button on every category (opens the Transactions page on it, with "Select all N to move, edit or delete"). The selection bar has Move (to any category or subcategory, or a new one, optionally remembering the merchant names), Edit (rename the merchant, change the type to spending, income, refund or transfer) and Delete (asks how many first; a payment counted toward a loan is un-counted first). 
- **Goals open up.** Click a goal (or a card or loan in the debt payoff planner) to see how many transactions count toward it, how much, and the list, 50 at a time; click a transaction to see it in its account.
- **Forecast month by month fixed.** The table was hidden behind a closed "Show" button, so the section looked empty. It is now always shown, and falls back to your plan when a saved what-if is gone.
- **Settings in sections.** A list (General, Appearance, Categories, Rules by amount, Accounts, People & setup, Remembered sources, Backup & data); each opens on its own with a "Back to Settings" link, and the Back button works.
- **Dashboard.** Insights moved to the bottom and show three, with "See all N insights".
- **Plainer wording on nine screens.** Review decisions say what they do ("just take it off this list"), Match my bank explains that only the starting balance changes, the Import sign tick-box says when to use it, Taxes & interest explains the ways to leave something out in one line, the backups folder is shown next to Restore (which is switched off while sample data is open), Accounts and Settings point to each other for Close and Delete, both "leave sample data" buttons have the same name, and the household budget explains category groups.
